## Historical correction

RC7 source `97da893062ebed1e27e9b35dbf0b68f248c12dd7`, tree
`db5e610c25eeda74f0ec0c931145249669eced69`, never produced canonical
production-signed packages. Its provisional checkpoint was invalidated after
the Tailscale Stop/Logout browser defect. Production building/signing and
downstream qualification were intentionally skipped. The owner confirms no
retained production-signed RC7 bundle exists to their knowledge; the bounded
archive search also found none.

The historical Phase 0 expectation of RC7-to-stable qualification was therefore
unrealized. Preserve that record and the failed checkpoint unchanged. This new
decision supersedes only the RC7 support/qualification expectation: the current
transition matrix marks RC7 unsupported, generated candidate and stable
manifests exclude it, and validators require that exclusion. An evidence-only
N/A would be insufficient because the prior manifest explicitly authorized it.

The real-package RC7 transition is non-applicable because there is no canonical
starting installation artifact. Source checks must still prove manifest
exclusion and fail-closed authorization through the unchanged updater library.
They must not manufacture, install, backfill or newly sign an RC7 package set,
and must not be described as a live RC7 installation test.

Every genuine supported path remains mandatory: all declared published stable
baselines, RC5, both distinct historical RC6 byte sets, RC14, RC15 and RC16.
RC18 remains unsupported; its live refusal before mutation remains required.
Version ordering does not grant transition support.

## Identity and evidence boundary

Release-scope section 4.6 requires a new RC for staging or verification-affecting
manifest changes. RC20 is assigned before generating corrected bytes; RC19 is
not patched or reused. Preserve its exact-source Tier 0/CI, independent A/B
builds, signatures and immutable artifact under their original identities.
None of those results qualifies RC20 or future stable bytes. RC19 did not
complete browser/VM qualification or achieve readiness for hardware.

RC20 requires fresh exact-source Tier 0/CI, independent clean reproducible
builds, production signatures and complete exact-byte browser/VM qualification.
A later stable promotion is limited to identity/publication metadata and must
repeat those gates on its own exact source and immutable bytes. Any required
runtime/backend/UI fix stops stable qualification and requires a new RC.

The earlier RC19 custody-not-satisfied statement records its historical stop
boundary. Current operator-authorized custody and recipient-key protection
checks pass, including two checksum-verified encrypted copies, one off-host,
and recovery to public fingerprint `d055711acf1d9a5b`. Reverify the private
assertions before signing. Keep private record contents, backup locations,
recipient private key and all decryption secrets outside repository and release
evidence; retain only sanitized pass/fail and an opaque record digest.

The package-only scope amendment, three-IPK deliverable, deferred firmware and
Factory production work, and mandatory physical canary remain unchanged.
No physical-router contact, merge, final tag, GitHub Release, Latest, discovery
or automatic rollout is authorized by this decision.
